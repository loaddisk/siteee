

> Alexa, play It’s All About Transient Ideology by Slowwves.

It is all temporary.

All of this.

The people we love. The places we call home. The things we possess. The ambitions we pursue. The versions of ourselves we cling to so desperately.

Even the sorrows that seem eternal will one day surrender to time.

We spend so much of our existence trying to preserve what was never meant to remain. We seek permanence in people, certainty in circumstances, and security in a world whose very nature is change.

And in doing so, we forget to live.

We suffer not because life is fleeting, but because we demand permanence from a world that promises none.

Yet consider this: would a sunset be as beautiful if it never ended? Would an embrace carry the same tenderness if we believed there would always be another?

It is precisely because things must pass that they deserve our deepest attention.

The passing of a moment does not diminish its worth. The falling of a leaf does not make its existence meaningless. Nor does the inevitable end of a life render the living of it insignificant.

Impermanence is not life’s cruelty. It is the very condition that gives life its urgency, its tenderness, and its beauty.

One day, you will look back and realise that the ordinary moments were the extraordinary ones.

The conversations you rushed. The laughter you barely noticed. The quiet mornings. The familiar faces. The warmth of another’s presence. The days you wished away in anticipation of something greater.

And you will understand that life was never waiting for you somewhere in the future.

It was happening all along.

So do not waste the present mourning what has passed, nor sacrifice it to the uncertainty of what has yet to come.

Do not postpone your happiness until circumstances obey your expectations. Do not withhold your affection as though time were an inexhaustible possession.

Love deeply, knowing nothing is guaranteed.

Hold close what matters, without believing you can hold it forever.

Accept change without allowing it to harden your heart.

And when a beautiful moment arrives, do not concern yourself with how long it might last. Simply be there to witness it.

For we are not here to conquer time, nor to escape the nature of our existence.

We are here, briefly, to experience it.

To feel. To love. To lose. To learn. To become. And eventually, to let go.

There is no tragedy in this, unless we spend our entire lives resisting it.

For life is not something we possess.

It is something we are momentarily privileged to experience.

And perhaps wisdom is nothing more than learning to stand within the passing moment, without demanding that it stay.

So live. Fully. Deliberately. Without postponement.

Because one day, all of this will be gone.

And that is not a reason to despair.

It is the greatest reason to be alive, right now.

For this moment is all we truly have.

And this moment is all we truly are.
